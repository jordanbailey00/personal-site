New Computer Modern fonts by Antonis Tsolomitis.
Upstream project: https://ctan.org/pkg/newcomputermodern

These WOFF2 files were copied without modification from Jeffrey Zhang's
Structure and Interpretation of Tensor Programs (SITP / asitpofborscht):
https://github.com/4thd1m/asitpofborscht/tree/29c1c6341bbe0ef9f68285ca0e8d2b7c632f8fb4/sitp/src/fonts

The reference's NewCM10-Regular and NewCM10-Italic browser conversions omit
malformed upstream Phonetic Extension glyphs; see the reference's
sitp/theme/fonts/fonts.css for the conversion notes and source provenance.

Font license information and exceptions are reproduced in LICENSE.txt.
The surrounding website code and text are independently authored.
The book's typography, layout, and palette were used as design references.

Goudy Initialen: original design by Frederic W. Goudy, digitized by Dieter
Steffmann, distributed on CTAN (https://ctan.org/pkg/initials) under LPPL.
The single-character WOFF2 conversions are copied without modification from
the same asitpofborscht commit listed above. The reference documents their
conversion in sitp/theme/fonts/fonts.css. License: https://www.latex-project.org/lppl/

The decorative rule in assets/decorations/ornament.svg is also copied from
that reference. Its embedded notice records the Vectorian/pgfornament
provenance and LPPL 1.3c-or-later license.
