visualiz3r lets you look inside the machines we use every day, in 3D, right in your browser. Rotate them, pull them apart, and click any piece to learn what it does. No screwdrivers, and no voided warranties.

What you can explore

  • Hardware: a graphics card, a processor, a memory stick, an SSD, a hard drive, and a Raspberry Pi, layer by layer, all the way down to the silicon.
  • A language model: a real (tiny) AI model running in the browser. Type a sentence and follow it through every step as the model predicts the next word, with a guided tour.
