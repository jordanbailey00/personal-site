Can an AI earn a fire cape? I built a simulator of RuneScape’s Fight Caves and trained an agent to survive all 63 waves, including the final boss, Jad. It learns by trying, failing, and trying again. Jad is not known for constructive feedback.

How it went

  • At its best evaluation, the agent defeated Jad in 94.9% of attempts in the simulator, with food and potions available.
  • The agent learned from scratch, without demonstrations or a scripted walkthrough.
  • A playable 3D viewer lets me watch the agent or take the controls myself.
