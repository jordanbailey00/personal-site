Byte World is a little fantasy RPG about exploring, finding better gear, and making questionable decisions around monsters. Play by typing commands in a terminal or using the browser interface. You can even joke your way past a goblin army. Finally, a practical use for my material.

What you can play

  • An adventure across 13 locations, with six bosses and an eight-stage main quest.
  • Turn-based fights, leveling, loot, and equipment upgrades.
  • The same game in the terminal and browser, with browser saves so you can pick up where you left off.
