RuneC is my take on rebuilding a RuneScape-style world: places to explore, characters wandering around, and game rules running underneath it all. The playable area centers on Varrock. A familiar city, with bugs I can now take personally.

What’s working

  • A 3D world covering 25 map regions, with 79 kinds of animated characters.
  • Movement, combat, and a viewer for exploring and inspecting the world.
  • The same game rules run with or without graphics, making it a foundation for future AI experiments.

It’s a work in progress, with more encounters and gameplay still to build.
